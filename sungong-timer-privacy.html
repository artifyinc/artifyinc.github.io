Privacy Policy — Pure Study (순공타이머)

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Pure Study (순공타이머, "the app") is a study timer for Android by Artify Inc.: one desk timer that counts down or up and keeps your study time for each day. This policy explains what data the app uses.

Summary

The timer works entirely on your phone, with no sign-up or login. Your timer and your daily study time stay on your phone (and in your own Android backup, if it is on). To fix crashes and see how the timer is used, the app sends usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase, tied to a random ID for this installation, never to your name, e-mail, phone number or precise location. The app does not use the Android advertising ID and shows no ads. In the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks first and sends nothing until you say yes. Anywhere, you can turn sending off and ask for your usage data to be deleted inside the app.

Data that stays on your phone

To work, the app keeps on the phone:

  • The timer: whether it is stopped, running or ringing, stopwatch or countdown, when the current run started, how much time it had, and the time you set;
  • Your study time for each day: the total study time of each date;
  • The mute switch: whether the button clicks and alarm sound are on;
  • Your choice about usage statistics, the last day whose study time range was sent (see below), and the ID usage statistics were last sent under, so you can still ask for that data to be deleted after you turn sending off.

The app does not send any of this to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

When a countdown ends while the app is closed, the "Time's up!" notification is made by the app on your phone; it does not come from a server.

Android backup

So that you keep your history when you change phones, your study time for each day and the mute switch are included in Android's backup to your Google account and in phone-to-phone transfer. The running timer and your choice about usage statistics are not included. This backup is made by Android and kept in your own Google account; we cannot see or access it. You can turn it off in Android settings (Google › Backup), and Google's terms apply to it.

Data sent to Google Firebase

The app uses two Google Firebase services: Analytics (usage statistics) and Crashlytics (crash reports). Nothing else in the app goes online.

The app never sends your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise location (it has no location permission), and never your exact study time. It does not collect the Android advertising ID: the advertising ID permissions are removed from the app, and the consent settings for advertising storage, ad user data and ad personalization are always "denied". The automatic screen-view report is turned off.

The data is sent encrypted (HTTPS) to Google, which processes it on our behalf as a service provider (processor). We do not sell data and do not share it with anyone for advertising. Where it is processed: Google may process this data on servers in the United States and other countries. For data from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's data processing terms for Firebase include the safeguards required for such transfers (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

How long it is kept

  • Usage statistics: up to 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Data on your phone and in your Android backup: until you delete it (see above).

Turning it off and deleting your data

Tap the shield button at the top right of the screen, next to the mute switch, to open Privacy:

  • Send usage statistics and crash reports (switch): turned off, the app stops sending usage statistics and crash reports, from that moment and every time it opens afterwards, and crash reports kept on the phone and not yet sent are deleted. The timer works exactly the same either way. You can turn it back on at any time.
  • Request deletion of my usage data: deletes from the phone the usage statistics and crash reports not yet sent and replaces the installation's IDs with new ones, so what was already sent is no longer linked to this phone. Your study time history is not touched.
    • Delete and request by e-mail: the app first opens your e-mail app with a ready message to our address containing only the old ID, then deletes. When you send it, we delete the usage statistics already sent under that ID from Google Analytics (Google may take a few days to complete it) and reply within one month. Crash reports already sent cannot be deleted one by one; they disappear at the end of the 90 days above.
    • This phone only: deletes on the phone only, without e-mail.

The data is not linked to your name or an account, so the ID in that message is the only way we can find it. If the app cannot open an e-mail app, it deletes nothing, so the old ID is not lost. Uninstalling the app also deletes the installation's IDs from the phone. For any other request, write to the e-mail address at the end of this page.

Users in the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (GDPR)

If the phone's SIM, mobile network, language region or time zone points to the EU, the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks on the first open whether it may send usage statistics, and sends nothing before you answer. "Send" and "Don't send" weigh the same, and the timer works the same with either. You can change your answer at any time with the switch above.

  • Controller: Artify Inc. (contact below).
  • Purpose and legal basis: usage statistics and crash reports are processed only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR / UK GDPR), to fix crashes and understand how the timer is used. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing done before.
  • Your rights: you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restriction of or objection to its processing, and a copy of it (portability), and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Use the controls above or write to us; we answer within one month. Because the data is tied only to a random installation ID, we may ask you to send that ID with the "Delete and request by e-mail" button so we can find it.
  • Complaints: you can complain to the data protection authority of the country where you live or work.

If you are under the age at which you can give this consent yourself in your country (16 in some EU countries), please ask a parent or guardian before choosing "Send", or choose "Don't send".

Messages you send

If you send us a deletion request or any other e-mail, we receive your e-mail address and the content of the message. We use them only to handle your request and reply, do not sell or share them, and delete them when you ask.

Permissions

  • Notifications: the "Time's up!" notification when a countdown ends while the app is closed.
  • Exact alarm: to ring at the exact second a countdown ends.
  • Vibration: the alarm vibrates in the rhythm of its beeps.
  • Internet and network state: only for Firebase (usage statistics and crash reports). The timer does not need the internet.

The app does not read your contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera or other apps.

Children

The app is meant for students aged 13 and over, is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The data described above is the same for everyone and never includes a name, contact details or precise location.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date. If the app starts sending new kinds of data, we will update this page before the app version that sends them is released.

개인정보 처리방침 — 순공타이머

최종 수정일: 2026년 11월 27일

순공타이머(이하 "앱")는 Artify Inc.가 만든 Android용 공부 타이머입니다. 책상 위 타이머 하나로 카운트다운이나 스톱워치를 쓰고, 날짜별 순공시간을 기록합니다. 이 방침은 앱이 어떤 데이터를 쓰는지 설명합니다.

요약

타이머는 가입이나 로그인 없이 폰 안에서만 동작합니다. 타이머와 날짜별 순공시간은 폰에(Android 백업을 켰다면 본인의 백업에도) 남습니다. 오류를 고치고 타이머가 어떻게 쓰이는지 알기 위해, 앱은 사용 통계와 오류(크래시) 보고를 Google Firebase로 보냅니다. 이 데이터는 이 설치의 무작위 식별자에만 연결되며, 이름·이메일·전화번호·정확한 위치와는 연결되지 않습니다. 앱은 Android 광고 ID를 쓰지 않고 광고도 없습니다. EU·EEA·영국·스위스에서는 먼저 묻고, 동의하기 전에는 아무것도 보내지 않습니다. 어디서나 앱 안에서 전송을 끄고 사용 데이터 삭제를 요청할 수 있습니다.

폰 안에만 두는 데이터

앱은 동작을 위해 다음을 폰에 저장합니다.

  • 타이머 상태: 멈춤·가는 중·울리는 중, 스톱워치·카운트다운, 지금 회차를 시작한 시각, 남은(쌓인) 시간, 맞춰 둔 시간
  • 날짜별 순공시간: 날짜마다의 순공시간 합계
  • 무음 설정: 버튼 소리와 알람음을 켰는지
  • 사용 통계 전송 여부, 순공시간 구간을 마지막으로 보낸 날(아래 참고), 그리고 사용 통계를 마지막으로 보낸 식별자(전송을 끈 뒤에도 그 데이터의 삭제를 요청할 수 있게)

이 데이터는 저희에게 보내지 않습니다. 앱을 지우거나 Android 설정에서 앱 데이터를 지우면 삭제됩니다.

앱이 닫힌 사이 카운트다운이 끝날 때 뜨는 "시간 끝!" 알림은 폰에서 앱이 직접 띄우는 것이며 서버에서 오지 않습니다.

Android 백업

폰을 바꿔도 기록이 남도록, 날짜별 순공시간과 무음 설정은 Android의 Google 계정 백업과 기기 간 데이터 이전에 포함됩니다. 달리던 타이머와 사용 통계 전송 여부는 포함되지 않습니다. 이 백업은 Android가 만들어 사용자 본인의 Google 계정에 보관하며, 저희는 보거나 접근할 수 없습니다. Android 설정(Google › 백업)에서 끌 수 있고, 백업에는 Google의 약관이 적용됩니다.

Google Firebase로 보내는 데이터

앱은 Google Firebase의 두 서비스, Analytics(사용 통계)와 Crashlytics(오류 보고)를 씁니다. 그 밖에 앱에서 인터넷으로 나가는 것은 없습니다.
